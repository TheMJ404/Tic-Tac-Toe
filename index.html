How to play

  • 1Choose Two Players to play with a friend on this device, or Vs Computer to play against the AI.
  • 2In Computer mode, pick a difficulty — Easy is forgiving, Hard barely ever loses.
  • 3Tap an empty cell to place your mark. X always goes first.
  • 4Get three of your marks in a row — across, down, or diagonally — to win.
  • 5Use New Game to clear the board any time. Scores keep counting until you reset them.
